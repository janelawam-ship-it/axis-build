Company profile

An e-store that sources, not a store that stocks.

Axis Build is not a conventional building materials store. We solve two problems that hold back good construction: limited availability of the right materials, and weak quality control. We find the finest materials from around the world and make sure you get exactly what your design calls for.

How it works

From your drawings to your site, in three steps.

  1. Step 1

    Send your design and schedule

    Share your house design and the schedule of materials that goes with it. Rough or complete, we work from what you have.

  2. Step 2

    We source every item

    Each material is matched to the producer and country that makes it best, then checked against your specification.

  3. Step 3

    Delivered, and right

    Your materials arrive complete and as specified, so your build keeps moving without substitutions or surprises.
